01

Overview & Core Privacy Commitment

TrueRep is built on the fundamental principle that health and athletic self-improvement should never compromise personal privacy.

Unlike traditional fitness platforms that require cloud sync, social profiles, and server-side video analysis, TrueRep is a privacy-first, edge-computed mobile application. All artificial intelligence models, computer vision pipelines, rep counters, velocity calculations, and database records execute locally on your physical smartphone.

ⓘ
Key Guarantee: TrueRep operates zero internal telemetry or telemetry backends. We do not maintain any user database, do not track your location, and cannot reconstruct your physical likeness.
02

Zero Account Architecture

You do not need to register, create a username, link a social media profile, or provide an email address to use TrueRep.

  • No Identity Association: Your workouts, body weight entries, streak days, levels, and rank points are tied strictly to an anonymous local instance installed on your device.
  • No Cross-Device Identity Sync: Because we operate no central login server, data does not sync to an external server unless you manually export a local backup file.
  • Immediate Access: You can launch workouts immediately after installation without completing authentication dialogs.
03

On-Device AI Vision & Biometrics

TrueRep features real-time rep counting and kinematic form analysis via device camera. Because camera feeds represent deeply sensitive personal imagery, we enforce the following architectural constraints:

Volatile RAM Processing Only

Camera frames are processed in volatile memory (RAM) by an embedded Google MediaPipe Pose ML model. As soon as body landmark coordinates (x, y, z) are extracted, the raw image frame is instantly destroyed.

Zero Cloud Video Uploads

At no point is any video stream, photograph, or frame buffered to disk or transmitted over the internet to TrueRep servers or third parties.

Mathematical Coordinates Only

Calculations such as rep completion, squat depth, and joint angle are derived strictly from normalized floating-point joint coordinates. No facial recognition or biometric identification is performed.

On-Demand Inspection

Workout reference videos and animations are stored locally inside the application package and play solely upon your request, eliminating unnecessary background processing and data transmission.

04

Nutrition Scanner & Open Food Facts

When you utilize TrueRep's dish scanner or barcode scanner to log meals, the app queries the public, open-source Open Food Facts API.

⚠
What is Transmitted: When you scan a product barcode, only the numeric barcode sequence (e.g. 8901234567890) is sent as an anonymous HTTP GET request to the Open Food Facts database. No user ID, IP tracking header, or personal identity is passed.

All nutritional databases accessed via Open Food Facts are licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL). Your meal logs and calorie totals are cached strictly inside your local device database.

05

Health Connect & Step Tracking

TrueRep integrates with Android's Health Connect API and native hardware step counters (pedometer sensor) to synchronize your daily activity metrics:

  • Permissions Requested: Read access for Step Count and Active Calories.
  • Purpose: To calculate your streak health, daily cardio milestones, and level progression in the app.
  • Zero Cloud Relaying: Data read from Health Connect is never uploaded to remote servers or shared with advertisers. It is rendered in your dashboard and stored locally in encrypted app storage.
  • User Control: You can revoke TrueRep's Health Connect permissions at any time via Android Settings → Security & Privacy → Health Connect.

07

Device Permissions Matrix

TrueRep adheres to the principle of least privilege. Permissions are requested only when required for core functionality:

android.permission.CAMERA Required for AI

Purpose: Live camera stream feed for MediaPipe Pose detection and optical barcode scanning.

Protection: Zero frames written to storage. Processing executes solely in volatile memory.

android.permission.ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION Optional

Purpose: Access to hardware step counter for daily step goals.

Protection: Used locally for XP calculation; never broadcasted externally.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional

Purpose: Daily workout reminders, rest timer alerts, and streak warnings.

Protection: Scheduled natively on the phone via Android AlarmManager without remote push notifications.

android.permission.INTERNET Network

Purpose: Google AdMob ad delivery and Open Food Facts barcode lookup.

Protection: No private workout or health data ever traverses this channel.

08

Local Storage & Data Retention

All your progress records are stored locally within your device's isolated application storage:

  • Completed Workouts & Rep Counts: Stored in high-performance local key-value databases (Hive / Isar / SQLite).
  • XP, Ranks & Coin Balances: Maintained on-device and rewarded upon completing exercises or watching optional ads.
  • Unlocked Cosmetics & Themes: Persisted in protected SharedPreferences / local storage.
  • Data Retention Duration: Your data remains stored on your device until you choose to uninstall the application or execute an in-app data reset.
09

Audio, Haptics & Voice Coaching

TrueRep includes real-time audio voice coaching cues (such as "Chest up", "Go lower", "Rep counted") and countdown timers:

  • On-Device Text-To-Speech (TTS): Audio is synthesized locally using Android's native TextToSpeech engine. No voice samples or microphone audio are recorded or analyzed.
  • Haptic Feedback: Device vibration motors provide tactile cues for rep finishes and timer completions with zero telemetry.

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

TrueRep is designed for general audiences and fitness enthusiasts. We do not knowingly market to or solicit data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

Because TrueRep does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials from any user, we do not have an active registry of child users. If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has installed the app, they can delete all local data by uninstalling the application or using the in-app wipe feature.

Your Data Rights & Total Deletion

Under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you possess the right to access, export, and delete your data at any time:

How to Completely Erase Your Data

Method 1

Via TrueRep Settings

Navigate to Settings → Data Management → Clear All App Data. This immediately purges all local databases, exercise history, and unlocked items.

Method 2

Via Android System Settings

Go to Settings → Apps → TrueRep → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage. This resets the application sandbox to factory default.

Method 3

Uninstall the Application

Uninstalling TrueRep triggers Android to permanently remove the app's isolated sandbox directory and all embedded databases.
